Design, development and day-to-day management of the website of Bushido Karaté du Valois, a karate, krav-maga and full contact club in Crépy-en-Valois: a lightweight static site with disciplines, coaches, weekly schedule, fees and downloadable registration forms, kept up to date for each season and self-hosted behind nginx. Also covers online registration with card payment in one or three installments through Monetico, and member management in OhayōDojo, with club emails sent from the bkv60.fr domain.
