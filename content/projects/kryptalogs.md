An end-to-end encrypted logging platform: logs are encrypted inside your application (ECDH P-256 + AES-256-GCM) before they leave it, and the server only ever stores ciphertext. Keys derive from a 24-word seed phrase and logs are decrypted in the browser. Drop-in SDKs for Go (slog), Python (logging) and Dart/Flutter, a dashboard with calendar browsing, and a free plan. Built in Go with Chi, Templ, HTMX and SQLite.
